About the event

From credit to capital

Despierta al Capital is a two-day financial education experience created by Capital Path to help the Latino community better understand credit, financial strategy, and the journey toward new opportunities.

Through education, expert perspectives, practical conversations, and strategic guidance, attendees will learn to look beyond a credit score and better understand the decisions that shape their financial path.

This is more than a credit event. It is a conversation about what comes next.

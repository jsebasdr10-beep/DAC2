Privacy & attendee data

Rehobot Strategy Corp. is responsible for the personal data collected for Despierta al Capital 2026.

What we collect

At registration: name, email, phone, and company or profession. We use this data to manage your attendance and send event information.

How consent is collected

  • We only share an attendee's contact details with a sponsor if that attendee gives explicit consent.
  • Consent is requested through a separate, optional checkbox at registration or at the sponsor's booth, naming the sponsor that will receive the data.
  • The checkbox is never pre-selected, and declining does not affect access to the event.

How data is protected

  • Data is stored in systems with restricted access, limited to the organizing team.
  • Sponsors receive only the contacts of attendees who consented, through a secure channel, and only for that sponsor.
  • Sponsors must use the data only to follow up on their own offering, may not sell or share it, and must honor any unsubscribe request.
  • We keep attendee data for up to 12 months after the event and then delete it, unless the attendee agrees otherwise.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction, or deletion of your data, or withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to ndiaz@rehobott.com.
